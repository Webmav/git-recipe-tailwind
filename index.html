Recipes

One-Pan Garlic Butter Pasta is a comforting dish that delivers rich, savory flavors with minimal cleanup. It features tender spaghetti coated in a velvety sauce made from melted butter, finely minced garlic, and freshly grated Parmesan cheese. A subtle hint of red pepper flakes adds gentle warmth, while fresh parsley offers a bright, herbaceous finish. Because the pasta cooks directly in a blend of broth and water, the natural starches bind the ingredients together into a naturally thick, luxurious coating without requiring heavy cream. It is the ultimate quick, satisfying weeknight dinner.
